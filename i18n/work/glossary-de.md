## Notes for consistent German

- Race plurals are inserted into sentences as they are; most of them end in -er, so the dative plural (-n) cannot be produced. Avoid the dative with a race name: use the accusative (gegen die {0}), the genitive (der {0}, Volk der {0}, Imperium der {0}) or a compound ({0}-Bake).
- Fleet names that appear after "Le" / "La" (automation message): "Le" → Der (pirate raid fleets, "Raid" must be masculine: Raubzug), "La" → Die (Reaper wave, "Vague" → Welle, feminine).
- Fleet and design names used elsewhere: Raid (pirate raid fleet) → Raubzug (masculine), Repaire (lair fleet) → Versteck, Vague (Reaper wave) → Welle, Porte-balise → Bakenträger, Colonisateur → Kolonisator, Éclaireur → Kundschafter, Cargo → Frachter, Citadelle → Zitadelle, Fortin → Fort, Mouilleur → Minenleger.
- Diplomatic proposals are inserted as accusative phrases: Frieden, ein Handelsabkommen, eine Forschungskooperation, ein Bündnis, einen Tribut, die Eröffnung von Botschaften (after "schlagen … vor", "lehnen … ab").
- Genitive phrases of the discovery stories ({de}): der Sonde, des Wracks, ... are genitive; the sentences use nouns that take a genitive (in der Umgebung {de}, im Herzen {de}, in der Nähe {de}).
- Plural conditionals: {1?en} = adds "en" when the French ending is not empty (Planet → Planeten, Jahr → Jahren); use `{n?plural|singular}` for irregular nouns (Wurmloch/Wurmlöcher, Fabrik/Fabriken).
