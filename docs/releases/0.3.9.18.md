## What's new in 0.3.9.18: the greenhouse effect, update notices

- **The greenhouse effect of industry** (a new Constellations rule; it replaces the random climate changes of 0.3.9.17, which had no cause): factories at work release greenhouse gases and their world slowly warms toward a level set by its industry, with a logarithmic response as with CO₂ (about +11 °C for 400 resources of factory output a year, +20 °C for 1,000, +32 °C for 2,500). It cools more slowly still once its industry gets cleaner, stops or is gone. Three technologies cut the emissions: Low-carbon energy (Energy 6, −40 %), Carbon capture (Biotechnology 8, half of the rest), Climate control (Biotechnology 14 and Energy 12, half again, and cooling five times faster). The warming shifts the natural temperature of the world: terraforming makes up for it within its limits, and a world too cold for your race can gain from it. The planet panel shows the warming reached and where it is heading. On in new games; it can be added to a game in progress in the game settings.
- **Ancient artifacts:** one free world in ten instead of one in three (5 empires found 77 of them in 100 years, far too many); a game created in 0.3.9.17 keeps three in ten of its own.
- **The Mystery Trader** now takes the minerals only: the fleet and its crew go home. In Stars! he kept the whole fleet, escorts included; a ship is worth little next to 5,000 kt of minerals, and no crew sails to its own loss.
- **Stargates:** a fleet that has to leave its cargo at the gate (a race without Gate Mastery) says so, and the ally whose gate received the minerals is told.
- **Update notices:** the copy of the game opened from your disk checks GitHub, twice a day at most, for a newer version; the opening screen and the top bar then offer it, with the notes of every version since yours (in French for French players, in English for the others) and the game to download in one file. It can be turned off in "Display and sound", which can also check right away. Every release now carries `index.html` on its own, next to the zip.
