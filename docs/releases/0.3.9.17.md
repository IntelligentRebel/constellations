## What's new in 0.3.9.17: tech trading, the Mystery Trader, artifacts, stargates and climate changes as in Stars!

- **Tech trading** (Stars! help and wiki): an empire may learn from the enemy ships it fought and survived, from the ships it takes apart at its stations and from the worlds it conquers. The chance is 0.5 × (1 − 0.5^n), n being the number of fields where that technology is ahead (so never more than 50 %), for one level in one of those fields, and only one such gain a year. Before, only conquest counted, and the roll was made field by field: over 80 % against an empire ahead everywhere. A level gained without research (trade, Mystery Trader, artifact) now moves research on to the next field set, like a level found by your labs.
- **The Mystery Trader**, after the Stars! FAQ and the table kept by craig-stars: he takes the whole fleet as payment, escorts included; an empire of 30 levels in all gets 6 levels for 5,000 kt and 10 for 9,800 kt (2 to 5 before), an empire of 120 levels a single one; each level falls in a random field (the lowest one time in four); when he offers a part, it is of the kind he announced; and he sometimes speeds up, now and then changing course (one chance in 25 a year).
- **Ancient artifacts**: planned in the code but never placed on the map, they now hide on a third of the unclaimed worlds when random events are on. The first colonists or troops to settle there find them: 100 to 400 research points in a random field (less for a colony under 1,000).
- **Stargates** ("Guts of Overgating"): the damage of a jump too long or too heavy adds to what the ships already had, and those pushed past their armor are lost (the jump order warns which ones would not make it); a fleet that just went through a gate is not repaired that year, and a station caught in a battle gives no repair bonus; a fleet of a race without Gate Mastery leaves its minerals at an ally's gate, and does not jump with colonists aboard.
- **Climate changes**, a random event of Stars!: about one year in twenty, a world's gravity, temperature or radiation shifts for good by 4 to 8 points, up or down (terraforming already done is kept). Colonies of over 5,000 are spared the first 20 years, and the owner is told what the world is now worth to its race.
