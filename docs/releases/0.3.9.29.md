## What's new in 0.3.9.29: long-range combat, honest battle plans, a transports view

Thanks to a player's feedback.

**Long-range ships keep their distance.** Torpedo and missile ships walked into the enemy's guns even when they were faster. Their damage does not fall with distance, so every square out of the enemy's reach looked the same to them, and the tie went to the square nearest the centre of the board, where the enemy came from. In a simulation of 4 faster torpedo ships against 4 phaser ships, they won 0 battles out of 60; now they win 46.

- Every tactic except "Maximize damage dealt" now weighs the damage it would take once the enemy has used the moves it has left in the round.
- A ship that outranges the enemy closes in only through squares the enemy cannot reach.
- It keeps a margin away from the edges of the board, where a ship keeping its distance gets cornered.
- A slower fleet still gets caught, as in Stars!.

**Battle plans explain themselves.**

- Each tactic now says what it does on the battle board.
- The plan says that design roles take precedence: artillery always keeps its distance and screens always charge, whatever tactic you pick.
- It warns about settings that contradict each other: "Disengage if challenged" with Retreat "never" still flees at the first hit.

**Battle reports say why.** When your long-range ships were caught, the report says so and gives the battle speeds (for example 1.25 squares per round against 0.5), and suggests the fix: faster engines, maneuvering jets or a lighter hull. It also names the fleets that fled because of their tactic.

**Transports view.** The Fleets page has a new Transports view showing, for each transport:

- how full its hold is and what it carries;
- the convoy or route it follows;
- where it is going and when it arrives;
- a box to put it in the logistics network or take it out.

The logistics network section of the Empire page links to it.

**Lighter rules.** A new "Lighter (fewer events)" button in the rules of a new game keeps the core of Constellations and removes the event layers. Off go the acts, dilemmas, long-running stories, cataclysms, pirates, intelligence, nemesis, greenhouse effect, the secret of the Ancients, the Portal relays and decoys.

**Route estimates.** Estimates now count the slingshot of a known black hole. Before, they announced one year too many for fleets leaving from near one.
