## What's new in 0.3.9.16: battles, mines and sieges as in Stars!

After an audit of the parity work done on craig-stars and of our own engine, checked against the Stars! help, its wiki and its release notes:

- **Armor damage is spread over the whole stack** (help, "Armor, Shields and Damage"). A ship is only destroyed when a hit exceeds the armor it has left; damaged ships go first, the rest of the damage is shared by all. Before, a stack's damage was pooled and every ship's worth of it killed a ship: stacks lost their ships, and their firepower, far too early, and a stack already at 50% lost half its ships to a 1-point hit. Fleets now come out of battles whole but battered.
- **Battles:** an attacked fleet fires back whatever its orders; breaking off costs seven squares of movement even when cornered; a torpedo miss that does no damage no longer triggers "Disengage if challenged"; at equal initiative the shortest range fires first, then an order drawn for the whole battle; the movement order follows mass ±15%; target classes follow the help; without combat roles, a stack of ten ships is no longer ten times less worth shooting than one ship alone. The cargo of destroyed ships is left where they died (salvage in space, on the planet in orbit), with a third of their hulls' minerals.
- **Mines** (Guts of Minefields): a mine's damage is shared by all the ships of the stack and the whole stack's shields absorb up to half of it (10 destroyers hit by a mine used to lose 5; they all survive now, at 100 damage each); a small fleet takes the minimum times its engines; overlapping fields of the same type are rolled once per light-year, in the order the fleet meets them; ships lost to mines leave their cargo behind.
- **Bombing:** all the bombers of an empire strike together once a year, against the defenses standing at the start of their strike (Stars! 2.70f: "multiple fleets bombing one planet act as one fleet"); smart bombs never kill the last 100 colonists.
- **Invasions:** every landing of a year on a world is settled together, the strongest side winning, instead of the oldest fleet; a world emptied by bombs can be taken by troops the same year, with its mines and factories.
- **Three loopholes closed:** loading cargo from another empire's fleet now needs a steal scanner; a waypoint on a foreign fleet goes where it was last seen once it is out of sight; colonists dropped by hand on an enemy world fight during the turn instead of at once (you could see the defenders, then undo).
