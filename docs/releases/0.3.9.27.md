## What's new in 0.3.9.27: spiral arms without an arc at the end

In a spiral galaxy made from its name, a star drawn past the edge of the disc was put on the edge. Since the winding grows with the distance, those stars lined up in a short arc at the end of each arm. Such a star is now drawn again, like a star too close to another one.

- About 2 % of the stars of a spiral move; the rest of the galaxy does not change.
- Games already started keep their map.
- Until Ron's generator gets the same one-line fix, those few stars differ from his.
