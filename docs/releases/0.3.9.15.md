## What's new in 0.3.9.15

- **Stars! tables corrected.** craig-stars, which the component and hull tables come from, fixed its data (thanks to sirgwain for the heads-up). Some costs had been read off a Humanoid starting game, so they were already miniaturized and Constellations miniaturized them a second time. Mini Bomber, Alien Miner, Mega Poly Shell, Langston Shell, Multi Contained Munition, Hush-a-Boom, Jump Gate, Trans-Galactic Mizer Scoop, Super Freighter, Heavy Dispenser 110 and Mine Dispenser 80 now cost what they cost in Stars!, and Total Terraform costs 70 instead of 100. Also corrected: Mini Gun, Ferret Scanner, Anti-Matter Pulverizer, the fuel tables of Settler's Delight and Enigma Pulsar, Super Cargo Pod, B-52 Bomber, Super Mine Layer, Meta Morph, Death Star, Battle Cruiser and Galleon. WM can't build Laser Battery, Planetary Shield or Neutron Shield, OBRM rules out the Midget Miner, Miner and Ultra-Miner hulls, and the transport cloak no longer fits a hull with room for weapons.
- **Starting fleets as in Stars!** SS gets a cloaked Shadow Transport (and a cloaked scout if it starts with the Stealth Cloak), WM starting at Construction 3 a Stalwart Defender, JoaT starting at tech 4 a Swashbuckler instead of the Teamster. No starting ship without the tech for its hull. PP and IT start with 25,000 colonists on tiny maps, where they get no second world.
- **Battles between several empires**: the starting squares depend on how many empires take part, as in Stars!.
- **Computers that only know what they see.** No difficulty level has ever had a production, growth or research bonus, but some decisions read the real game state. Now they judge an enemy fleet by the designs they have met in battle (otherwise by its hull, or by its mass for a contact they can't make out), plan invasions from the defenses they have counted, and read other empires' scores only when the scores are public or their spies have estimated them. Measured over 20 games of 100 years at Expert level against the 0.3.9.14 AI: no measurable loss (50.1% of the total score, 33.5 planets against 32.9, 132 conquests against 130).
