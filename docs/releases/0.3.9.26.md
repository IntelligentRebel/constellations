## What's new in 0.3.9.26: a whole galaxy from its name

A new choice in **Galaxy region** (New game), next to the others, which all stay: **Whole galaxy, from its name**, made with **Rick's galaxy generator**.

- Type a name (« Galaxy1 »): the generator draws from it a spiral, elliptical, irregular, lenticular or ring galaxy. The new game window says which (« Galaxy1: spiral galaxy with 5 arms, 2,000 stars »).
- It is reproduced number for number: the same name gives the same galaxy as in Rick's generator, and the worlds of the game are its first stars that have planets, at least 14 light-years apart. You can find each of them in his generator (the system view gives its catalogue number).
- An « Even spread » box gives a less dense core.
- Unexplored stars show their spectral type by their colour (toned down, so they are never taken for an empire's).
- As in Stars!, each star is a system summed up in one world. A right click, **View the system** (or the ☉ button of the planet panel), shows the star, its habitable zone, its planets and their moons, and the main world: the one the game's values describe. Nothing there is managed: it shows what the game sums up.

Spirals read best from a Medium universe up.
